Commission Engine

Every deal structure, handled.

No two affiliate deals are the same. Affiliar's commission engine handles them all — without custom code, spreadsheets, or month-end panic.

Revenue Share

Earn a % of NGR (or GGR). Configure negative carryover rules per affiliate.

CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)

Fixed payout per qualifying FTD. Configurable fraud gates: min deposit, hold period, wager threshold (flat or Nx), cash-retention floor. Pending FTDs promote automatically on recalc.

Hybrid (RevShare + CPA)

Combine both models. CPA on acquisition plus ongoing RevShare.

Refer-a-Friend

Player-to-player rewards, pulled on demand.

Configure the reward, set the gates — Affiliar handles attribution and qualification. Your casino pulls earned rewards when the player opens their "My Rewards" page, credits the wallet, and acks. No webhooks to host, no retries to worry about.

Step 1

Player invites a friend

Casino sends track-signup when the friend registers with the code.

Step 2

Friend signs up & deposits

Affiliar runs hold period, wager threshold, and monthly cap gates; qualifies and queues both rewards.

Step 3

Casino pulls & credits

Player opens "My Rewards" → your backend hits /rewards, credits the wallet, posts /claim to ack.
