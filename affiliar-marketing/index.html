Commission Engine

Every deal structure, handled.

No two affiliate deals are the same. Affiliar's commission engine handles them all — without custom code, spreadsheets, or month-end panic.

Revenue Share

Earn a % of NGR (or GGR). Configure negative carryover rules per affiliate.

CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)

Fixed payout per qualifying FTD. Set minimum deposit thresholds.

Hybrid (RevShare + CPA)

Combine both models. CPA on acquisition plus ongoing RevShare.
