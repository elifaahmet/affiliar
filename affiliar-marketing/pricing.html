Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us.

How does billing work?
Plans are billed monthly in USD. You can pay by credit card, wire transfer, or SEPA direct debit. Invoices are generated at the start of each billing cycle and are available in your dashboard. Annual billing is available at a 15% discount — contact sales for details.
Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. When upgrading, the new features are available immediately and we prorate the remaining days. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
What happens if I cancel?
You can cancel anytime with no penalty. Your account stays active until the end of the current billing period. After that, access is suspended but your data is retained for 90 days, giving you time to export or reactivate.
Can I export my data?
Absolutely. All plans include full data export in CSV and JSON formats. You can export affiliates, commission reports, activity logs, and payment history at any time from your dashboard or via the REST API.
Do you offer custom or enterprise plans?
Yes. If you need custom affiliate limits, dedicated infrastructure, SLA guarantees, or on-premise deployment, contact us and we'll put together a plan that fits your needs.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. All plans come with a 14-day free trial so you can evaluate the platform before committing. You get full access to all plan features during the trial. Contact us to get started.
