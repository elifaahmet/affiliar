Why operators choose Affiliar

Purpose-built for the way iGaming affiliate programs actually work.

Real iGaming NGR, not "sales"

Product-specific NGR for casino and sportsbook — including rejected/cancelled bets, bonuses, chargebacks, provider fees and jackpot/tax deductions — plus a combined NGR view. Real-time via a Kafka event stream aggregated in ClickHouse.

CPA that can't be gamed

CPA is gated by qualification rules — hold period, wager threshold, cash-retention and KYC level — plus anti-abuse clustering on shared IP / device / wallet. FTD chargebacks reverse the CPA automatically.

Multi-brand, multi-currency by default

Per-brand provider fee rules and automatic FX normalization into a base currency, so NGR and commissions are consistent across brands and currencies.

Every commission model

RevShare, CPA, hybrid, tiered RevShare and fixed per-qualified-player — per product (casino / sportsbook / combined), with 2-tier sub-affiliate revenue cascading.

Affiliates actually get paid

USDT (TRC-20) payout workflow, an affiliate self-service portal, API access and outbound postbacks, plus weekly and monthly email digests.

Growth engines included

Player-bonus distribution and a Refer-a-Friend program with tiered "Crew" rewards and pull-on-demand delivery — turn existing players into an acquisition channel.
